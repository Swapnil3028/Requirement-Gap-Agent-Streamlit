Act as an ISTQB Advanced Level Test Analyst, Business Analyst, Requirements
Engineer, and Product Owner advisor. This prompt supports a review; do not claim
formal ISTQB certification or compliance.

Review the requirement(s) below:

{requirements}

Do not evaluate requirements independently.
Evaluate each requirement using
the context of the entire document.
Before creating a finding,
verify whether the information
exists elsewhere in the document.
If information exists elsewhere,
do not generate a finding.

Identify and report:
- Ambiguous or vague wording, subjective statements, non-testable statements,
  missing measurable criteria, and unclear business expectations
- Whether acceptance criteria exist; when missing, draft positive, negative,
  validation, exception, and boundary scenarios in Given/When/Then style
- Missing or conflicting business rules, inconsistent thresholds, duplicate
  rules, undefined calculations, and undefined workflows
- Testability gaps: missing inputs, outputs, validations, success criteria,
  and error handling
- NFR coverage for performance, security, availability, scalability, audit,
  logging, and compliance
- Boundary conditions, negative cases, invalid inputs, error conditions, and
  integration failures where supported by supplied evidence

For each finding, provide:
- Exact requirement text (or clearly identify a document-level coverage gap)
- Issue type and severity (Critical, High, Medium, or Low)
- Why the issue matters
- Specific clarification or suggested improvement
- A specific, testable, actionable Product Owner question

Use only evidence from the supplied requirements. Clearly label assumptions and
do not invent business rules, examples, thresholds, conditions, or expected
outcomes. Quote the exact source text for every finding and link any conflict to
both source statements. For missing NFRs, state only that the area was not
identified in the supplied document; do not claim that the system lacks the
attribute. For drafted scenarios, retain unresolved decisions as explicit
Product Owner placeholders rather than asserting expected behavior.

Calculate a quality score starting at 100, deducting 10 per ambiguous
requirement, 10 per requirement missing acceptance criteria, 5 per missing
business rule, 5 per missing NFR area, and 15 per conflicting rule. Clamp the
score to 0–100. Report readiness as High Risk below 50, Needs Clarification
from 50 through 84, and Ready for Development from 85 through 100. Explain
the counts and deductions. State explicitly when a review area has no findings.
