incomplete, draft only criteria and scenarios directly supported by the supplied

the expected outcome or a condition is missing, leave it unresolved and ask the
Product Owner rather than inventing an answer.

- Give it a concise, test-focused title.
- State observable preconditions, action, and expected outcome.
- Cover only behavior supported by the requirement.
- Mark unresolved policy or business decisions as questions for the Product
  Owner instead of assuming an answer. If no testable outcome is specified, do
  not present the scenario as a complete acceptance criterion.
- When a recognizable actor and action are present, provide positive, negative,
  validation, exception, and boundary scenario drafts; unresolved conditions
  and results must remain explicit Product Owner placeholders.
