Check whether each requirement includes non-empty acceptance criteria or an
explicit Given/When/Then scenario. An empty acceptance-criteria spreadsheet
cell is not evidence that criteria exist. When criteria are missing and the
source supports a recognizable actor and action, draft positive, negative,
validation, exception, and boundary scenarios in Given/When/Then style.

Do not evaluate requirements independently.
Evaluate each requirement using
the context of the entire document.
Before creating a finding,
verify whether the information
exists elsewhere in the document.
If information exists elsewhere,
do not generate a finding.

Use only actors, actions, inputs, and conditions stated in the requirement.
Keep every unspecified precondition, expected result, rejection behavior,
validation rule, exception, and boundary as an explicit Product Owner
placeholder; never invent business behavior. State whether the expected
outcome is present, missing, or incomplete, and ask a specific question for
every unresolved decision.
